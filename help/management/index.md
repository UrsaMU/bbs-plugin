BBS — MANAGEMENT

Commands for editing, moving, stickying, and flagging posts.

COMMANDS
  +bbremove <#>/<post>[,...]   Delete one or more posts.
  +bbmove <#>/<post> to <#>   Move a post to another board.
  +bbedit <#>/<post>[=<o>/<n>] Edit a post or load it as draft.
  +bbsticky <#>/<post>         Toggle sticky (pinned) status.
  +bbflag <#>/<post>[=<reason>] Flag a post for review.

SEE ALSO: +help bbs
