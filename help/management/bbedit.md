+BBEDIT

Inline-edit a BBS post or reply with **+bbedit**.

SYNTAX
  +bbedit <#>/<post>[.<reply>][=<old>/<new>]

EXAMPLES
  +bbedit 2/3=typo/fixed       Replace "typo" with "fixed" in post 3.
  +bbedit 2/3.1=typo/fixed     Replace text in reply 1 of post 3.
  +bbedit 2/3                  Load post 3 into draft for full editing.

SEE ALSO: +help bbpost, +help bbremove, +help bblink
