BBS — BULLETIN BOARD SYSTEM

The BBS provides persistent message boards for IC and OOC communication.

TOPICS
  +help bbs reading      Reading posts and boards.
  +help bbs posting      Writing posts, drafts, and replies.
  +help bbs social       Subscriptions, notifications, and search.
  +help bbs management   Editing, moving, and flagging posts.
  +help bbs staff        Staff board and moderation tools.

QUICK START
  +bblist                See all boards.
  +bbread                Read all boards.
  +bbpost <#>=<subj>/<body>  Post to a board.

SEE ALSO: +help bbread, +help bbpost, +help bblist
