BBS — SOCIAL

Commands for subscriptions, notifications, signatures, and search.

COMMANDS
  +bblist                      List all boards.
  +bbjoin <#>                  Subscribe to a board.
  +bbleave <#>                 Unsubscribe from a board.
  +bbnotify <#>=<on|off>       Toggle new-post notifications.
  +bbwatch <#>/<post>          Watch a post for replies.
  +bbsig [<text>]              Set or clear your signature.
  +bbsearch <#>/<query>        Search posts by author or tag.

SEE ALSO: +help bbs
