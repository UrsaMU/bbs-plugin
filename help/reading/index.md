BBS — READING

Commands for reading boards and tracking unread posts.

COMMANDS
  +bbread [<#>[/<posts>]]   Read boards and posts.
  +bbnext [<#>]             Read your next unread message.
  +bbcatchup [<#>|all]      Mark boards as fully read.
  +bbunread [<#>]           Mark boards as unread.

SEE ALSO: +help bbs
