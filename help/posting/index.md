BBS — POSTING

Commands for writing posts, managing drafts, and replying.

COMMANDS
  +bbpost [<#>/<subj>[=<body>]]   Start or submit a post.
  +bb <text>                      Append text to your draft.
  +bbproof                        Preview your current draft.
  +bbtoss                         Discard your current draft.
  +bbreply [<#>/<post>[=<text>]]  Reply to a post.
  +bbtag [<tag>[,<tag>...]]       Set tags on your draft.
  +bblink <#>/<post>[=<sceneId>]  Link a scene to a post.

SEE ALSO: +help bbs
